Figure 5. CTN-0094, test patients: the two no-skill forecasts under four scorings. Rows are the nine measures (sensitivity and specificity share one row); Supplement Table S2 gives the values. Filled symbols are scores and open symbols baselines (circles: shuffled rate so far; triangles: run-in rate), and colored segments show the excess (green: score above baseline; orange: below). The first two columns read pooled and per-patient scores against the usual baselines of Section 2.3. The third column reads per-patient scores against the patient's own event rate over the scored weeks, a baseline that peeks at the scored outcomes; for Brier skill it shows the skill against the own rate as the reference forecast (baseline 0). The own rate does not enter the AUC, sensitivity plus specificity or the calibration slope. The fourth column reads each per-patient score against its shuffled baseline; as in Table 5, the PPV, NPV, net benefit and Brier skill are taken against the own rate, which a within-patient shuffle leaves unchanged. Small squares and diamonds give the scores in REPLACE-BG and the College Experience Study on the AUC and the calibration slope.
